Started as giza cables over in 1984, we have grown into giza power industry (gpi), a regional, diversified integrated cable solutions provider.

STARTED AS GIZA CABLES OVER IN 1984, WE HAVE GROWN INTO GIZA POWER INDUSTRY (GPI), A REGIONAL, DIVERSIFIED INTEGRATED CABLE SOLUTIONS PROVIDER.

Built since 1984 on a total area of 81,745 m2 at the industrial area of Abo Rawash, GPI is today one of the best modern factories in the region to manufacture and supply electrical cables with cutting-edge technology and unparalleled quality serving diverse industries.

GPI's Cables portfolio provides a comprehensive range of integrated cable solutions that comply with international standards and are recognized locally and globally. Thanks to our efficient manufacturing process and state-of-the-art quality labs, we boast our ability to provide reliable, cost-effective, high-quality products and innovative solutions for our customers.

With a growing reach and an annual production Capacity of Approximately 25,000 tons, we offer Power, special cables and OHTL conductors.
