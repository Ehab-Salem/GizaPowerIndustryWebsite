GPI Acquires BASEC Certification for Medium Voltage Cables

23 Nov 2025 Certifications

Introduction

Giza Power Industry (GPI) is proud to announce the successful acquisition of the BASEC certification for its Medium Voltage (MV) cable range. This achievement showcases GPI's unwavering commitment to delivering globally certified, high-performance cable solutions that meet the highest safety and quality benchmarks.

What Does This Certification Mean?

BASEC (British Approvals Service for Cables) is one of the world's most recognized and respected certification bodies, specializing in rigorous testing and approval of cable quality.

GPI's MV cables underwent extensive evaluation covering electrical performance, material durability, construction integrity, and long-term operational reliability.

Achieving this certification demonstrates that GPI's cables consistently meet or exceed international manufacturing standards.

Strengthening Global Market Presence

With this certification, GPI reinforces its strong positioning in the European, Middle Eastern, and African markets—especially the United Kingdom, where BASEC compliance is a critical requirement.

This milestone strengthens customer confidence, expands export opportunities, and reflects GPI's dedication to manufacturing excellence.

GPI's Commitment to Quality

GPI continues to invest heavily in state-of-the-art production lines, comprehensive quality labs, and continuous improvement programs.

Through stringent testing protocols—including electrical, mechanical, and environmental tests—GPI ensures that every meter of cable delivered to customers aligns with global safety and performance standards.

Looking Ahead

This certification is not the end—it's a key milestone in GPI's strategy to expand its international footprint and deliver advanced, sustainable, and highly reliable power solutions.

By integrating world-class quality assurance systems and pioneering industry practices, GPI aims to continue shaping the future of the cable manufacturing sector.
