Single Core Copper & Aluminum Conductors

Soft annealed Stranded circular compacted Copper or Stranded circular compacted Aluminum conductor, semiconducting layer as conductor screen, XLPE insulated semiconducting layer as non metallic insulation screen, Copper tape or wire as metallic insulation screen, and PVC sheathed. Soft annealed Stranded circular compacted Copper or Stranded circular compacted Aluminum conductor, semiconducting layer as conductor screen, XLPE insulated semiconducting layer as non metallic insulation screen, Copper tape or wire as metallic insulation screen, and PVC sheathed.

These cables are generally suitable for direct burial or for installation on trays or in ducts.

Relevant Standard
IEC 60502
Conductor
Plain copper conductor class 2 or Aluminum conductor class 2
Conductor Screen
Extruded Semiconductive Compound
Insulation
Extruded XLPE Compound Rated 90°C
Insulation Screen

Non-Metallic part: Extruded Semi conductive Compound (Bonded or Strippable) to the insulation.

Metallic part: One of the following:

  • Copper Tape Applied Helically With Suitable Overlap
  • Copper Wires Helically Applied And Bonded With A Copper Tape To Achieve Electrical Contact
  • Overlapped Copper Tape Then Copper Wires (Available In Single Core Only)

Conductor screen, XLPE insulation, and non-metallic insulation screen are applied simultaneously using a triple head extruder.

Outer Sheath
