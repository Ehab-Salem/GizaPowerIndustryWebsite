Electrical Design
Engineer

Job Summary

We are looking for an Electrical Design Engineer to support the development, design, and documentation of cable systems and power solutions. The ideal candidate will contribute to engineering activities while ensuring compliance with international standards.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering
  • 1-3 Years Of Relevant Experience In Electrical Design Or Cable Manufacturing
  • Strong Understanding Of LV/MV Cable Systems
  • Proficiency In CAD Tools (e.g., AutoCAD, EPLAN)
  • Strong Analytical And Problem-Solving Skills
  • Good Communication And Teamwork Abilities

Responsibilities

  • Prepare Electrical Design Drawings, Calculations, And Technical Documents.
  • Conduct Feasibility Analyses And Cable Sizing Based On Project Requirements.
  • Support Production And Quality Teams With Engineering Input.
  • Ensure Compliance With IEC, BS, And BASEC Standards.
  • Collaborate With Project Managers To Deliver Effective Technical Solutions.
  • Participate In Design Reviews And Continuous Improvement Initiatives.

Preferred Skills

Teamwork Technical Reports Communication Analysis Skills Communication Analysis Skills Analysis Skills Teamwork
